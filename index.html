📈 Progressive Overload

How to Progress Each Week

  • Week 1-2: Learn movements, focus on form with moderate weight
  • Week 3-4: Once form is solid, start pushing intensity
  • Every week: Try to add weight OR reps to main lifts
  • If you complete all reps with good form, add 2.5-5kg next session
  • Track all workouts in a notebook or app
  • Don't sacrifice form for weight - ego lifting leads to injury

🍗 Nutrition for Muscle Gain

Calories

Aim for 200-500 calorie surplus above maintenance. For you (97kg): Start with 2,800-3,000 calories/day and adjust based on weekly weight changes. Gain 0.25-0.5kg per week.

Protein

0.8-1g per pound of bodyweight. For you: 170-215g protein daily. Sources: chicken, fish, lean beef, eggs, Greek yogurt, protein powder.

Carbs & Fats

Carbs: 3-5g per kg bodyweight (290-485g) for energy. Fats: 0.5-0.8g per kg (49-78g) for hormones. Fill remaining calories with healthy foods.

Meal Timing

Eat protein within 2 hours post-workout. Space protein across 3-5 meals. Pre-workout: carbs + protein 1-2 hours before. Stay hydrated: 3-4L water daily.

💤 Recovery & Rest

Sleep

7-9 hours per night is critical. Muscle grows during sleep, not during workouts. Poor sleep = poor gains.

Rest Days

Take at least 2 full rest days per week. Active recovery (walking, light cardio) is fine but avoid heavy lifting.

Deload Weeks

Every 6-8 weeks, reduce weight by 40-50% for one week. This allows full recovery and prevents burnout.

Listen to Your Body

Sharp pain = stop immediately. Soreness = normal. If constantly exhausted or joints hurt, take extra rest days.

🎯 Final Tips for Success

✅ Be Consistent

The best program is the one you stick to. Show up, even on days you don't feel like it.

✅ Track Progress

Take weekly photos, measurements, and track strength gains. Scale weight is only one metric.

✅ Form First, Weight Second

Perfect form with lighter weight beats heavy weight with poor form every time.

✅ Be Patient

Building muscle takes months and years, not weeks. Trust the process and stay committed.
