3-Day Full Body Split

Schedule: Monday, Wednesday, Friday (or any 3 non-consecutive days)

Duration: 60-75 minutes per session

🔥 Warm-Up (10 minutes before each workout)

• 5 min light cardio (treadmill, bike, or jumping jacks)

• Dynamic stretches: arm circles, leg swings, torso twists

• 1-2 warm-up sets of first exercise with light weight

Day 1, 3, 5: Full Body A/B Alternating

60-75 min

Workout A (Week 1: Mon/Fri, Week 2: Wed)

1. Barbell Squat 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Form: Feet shoulder-width, knees track over toes, chest up, go to parallel or below
Rest: 2-3 minutes between sets
2. Bench Press 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Form: Arch your back slightly, retract shoulder blades, bar touches mid-chest
Rest: 2-3 minutes
3. Bent-Over Barbell Row 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Form: Hinge at hips, back straight, pull to lower chest, squeeze shoulder blades
Rest: 2 minutes
4. Overhead Press 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Form: Core tight, press straight up, don't arch back excessively
Rest: 2 minutes
5. Romanian Deadlift 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Form: Slight knee bend, hinge at hips, bar stays close to legs, feel hamstrings stretch
Rest: 2 minutes
6. Dumbbell Bicep Curls 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Form: Elbows stay at sides, controlled movement, squeeze at top
Rest: 1-1.5 minutes
7. Plank 3 sets x 30-60 sec
Form: Body in straight line, core tight, don't let hips sag
Rest: 1 minute

Workout B (Week 1: Wed, Week 2: Mon/Fri)

1. Deadlift 3 sets x 6-8 reps
Form: Feet hip-width, back straight, drive through heels, hips and shoulders rise together
Rest: 3 minutes
2. Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Form: Bench at 30-45 degrees, dumbbells at chest level, press up and slightly together
Rest: 2 minutes
3. Pull-Ups or Lat Pulldown 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Form: Pull with elbows, not biceps. Chest to bar for pull-ups
Alternative: Use assisted pull-up machine or lat pulldown if can't do pull-ups yet
Rest: 2 minutes
4. Leg Press 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Form: Feet shoulder-width, don't let knees cave in, full range of motion
Rest: 2 minutes
5. Dumbbell Shoulder Press 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Form: Seated or standing, press dumbbells up and slightly together
Rest: 2 minutes
6. Tricep Rope Pushdown 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Form: Elbows tucked at sides, extend down fully, squeeze triceps
Rest: 1-1.5 minutes
7. Hanging Leg Raises 3 sets x 10-15 reps
Form: Hang from bar, raise legs to 90 degrees, control the descent
Alternative: Lying leg raises if hanging is too difficult
Rest: 1 minute

4-Day Upper/Lower Split

Schedule: Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri or Mon/Wed/Thu/Sat

Duration: 60-75 minutes per session

🔥 Warm-Up (10 minutes before each workout)

• 5 min light cardio

• Dynamic stretches specific to workout (upper or lower body)

• 1-2 warm-up sets of first exercise

Day 1: Upper Body A

Chest, Back, Shoulders
1. Bench Press 4 sets x 6-8 reps
Heavy compound movement. Rest 2-3 minutes.
2. Bent-Over Barbell Row 4 sets x 6-8 reps
Pull to lower chest, squeeze shoulder blades. Rest 2-3 minutes.
3. Overhead Press 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Strict form, core tight. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Lat Pulldown 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Pull to upper chest, squeeze back. Rest 1.5 minutes.
5. Dumbbell Lateral Raise 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Light weight, focus on shoulders. Rest 1 minute.
6. Face Pulls 3 sets x 15-20 reps
Great for rear delts and shoulder health. Rest 1 minute.

Day 2: Lower Body A

Quads, Glutes, Hamstrings
1. Barbell Squat 4 sets x 6-8 reps
King of leg exercises. Go deep, stay tight. Rest 3 minutes.
2. Romanian Deadlift 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Feel the hamstring stretch. Rest 2 minutes.
3. Leg Press 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Deep range of motion. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Leg Curl 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Isolated hamstring work. Rest 1.5 minutes.
5. Calf Raises 4 sets x 15-20 reps
Full range of motion, pause at top. Rest 1 minute.
6. Planks 3 sets x 45-60 sec
Core stability. Rest 1 minute.

Day 3: Upper Body B

Chest, Back, Arms
1. Incline Dumbbell Press 4 sets x 8-10 reps
Upper chest focus. Rest 2 minutes.
2. Pull-Ups or Lat Pulldown 4 sets x 8-10 reps
Use assisted machine if needed. Rest 2 minutes.
3. Dumbbell Shoulder Press 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Seated for stability. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Cable Rows 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Squeeze shoulder blades together. Rest 1.5 minutes.
5. Barbell Bicep Curl 3 sets x 10-12 reps
No swinging, controlled movement. Rest 1.5 minutes.
6. Tricep Dips or Pushdowns 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Full extension at bottom. Rest 1.5 minutes.

Day 4: Lower Body B

Legs, Glutes, Core
1. Deadlift 4 sets x 5-6 reps
Heavy and powerful. Perfect form essential. Rest 3 minutes.
2. Front Squat or Goblet Squat 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Quad-focused variation. Rest 2 minutes.
3. Bulgarian Split Squat 3 sets x 10-12 reps/leg
Unilateral leg development. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Leg Extension 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Quad isolation, squeeze at top. Rest 1.5 minutes.
5. Glute-Ham Raise or Leg Curl 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Hamstring and glute builder. Rest 2 minutes.
6. Ab Wheel Rollouts 3 sets x 10-15 reps
Advanced core exercise. Use knees if needed. Rest 1 minute.

5-Day Push/Pull/Legs Split

Schedule: Mon (Push), Tue (Pull), Wed (Legs), Thu (Push), Fri (Pull), Sat/Sun (Rest)

Duration: 60-75 minutes per session

🔥 Warm-Up (10 minutes before each workout)

• 5 min light cardio

• Specific warm-up for muscle groups being trained

• 2 warm-up sets of first exercise

Day 1 & 4: Push (Chest, Shoulders, Triceps)

Push Muscles
1. Bench Press 4 sets x 6-8 reps
Main chest builder. Heavy weight, perfect form. Rest 2-3 minutes.
2. Overhead Press 4 sets x 6-8 reps
Primary shoulder exercise. Rest 2-3 minutes.
3. Incline Dumbbell Press 3 sets x 8-10 reps
Upper chest focus. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Dumbbell Lateral Raise 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Side delt builder. Lighter weight, higher reps. Rest 1.5 minutes.
5. Chest Dips or Cable Flyes 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Lower chest emphasis. Rest 1.5 minutes.
6. Tricep Rope Pushdown 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Isolated tricep work. Rest 1 minute.
7. Overhead Tricep Extension 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Long head of triceps. Rest 1 minute.

Day 2 & 5: Pull (Back, Biceps)

Pull Muscles
1. Deadlift (Day 2 only) 4 sets x 5-6 reps
King of back exercises. Only on first pull day. Rest 3 minutes.
2. Pull-Ups or Lat Pulldown 4 sets x 8-10 reps
Lat width builder. Rest 2 minutes.
3. Bent-Over Barbell Row 4 sets x 8-10 reps
Back thickness. Pull to lower chest. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Seated Cable Row 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Mid-back focus. Squeeze shoulder blades. Rest 2 minutes.
5. Face Pulls 3 sets x 15-20 reps
Rear delts and upper back. Rest 1 minute.
6. Barbell Bicep Curl 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Mass builder for biceps. No swinging. Rest 1.5 minutes.
7. Hammer Curls 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Brachialis and forearm development. Rest 1 minute.

Day 3: Legs (Quads, Hamstrings, Glutes, Calves)

Leg Day
1. Barbell Squat 4 sets x 6-8 reps
King of leg exercises. Go deep, stay tight. Rest 3 minutes.
2. Romanian Deadlift 4 sets x 8-10 reps
Hamstring and glute builder. Feel the stretch. Rest 2 minutes.
3. Leg Press 3 sets x 10-12 reps
Additional quad volume. Rest 2 minutes.
4. Leg Curl 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Hamstring isolation. Rest 1.5 minutes.
5. Leg Extension 3 sets x 12-15 reps
Quad isolation. Squeeze at top. Rest 1.5 minutes.
6. Calf Raises (Standing) 4 sets x 15-20 reps
Full stretch and contraction. Rest 1 minute.
7. Seated Calf Raises 3 sets x 15-20 reps
Targets soleus muscle. Rest 1 minute.

📈 Progressive Overload: The Key to Growth

How to Progress Each Week

  • Week 1-2: Learn the movements, focus on perfect form with moderate weight
  • Week 3-4: Once form is solid, start pushing intensity
  • Every week after: Try to add weight OR reps to your main lifts
